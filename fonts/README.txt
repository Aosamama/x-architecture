TeXGyreAdventor-Regular.woff2 / TeXGyreAdventor-Bold.woff2

TeX Gyre Adventor, a free clone of ITC Avant Garde Gothic, the design that Century Gothic follows.
Used here as the free stand-in for Century Gothic (which is a commercial font and cannot be hosted),
for Latin text only. The files are the unmodified original OpenType fonts converted to WOFF2
(lossless re-packaging, no change to the glyphs).
